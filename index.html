How this works
How values work. Values blend five trade value sources, each scaled so its top player is 100, then averaged.
Power rankings. Best starting lineup (QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, TE, flex) plus a quarter of the top three bench players. Records don't count. The score puts the top team at 100 and everyone else relative to them.
How the verdict works. A player is worth what he's worth above the best free agent, since anyone could pick that guy up for nothing. Stars count for more than the same total in smaller pieces (two 30s don't buy a 60), and waiver-level throw-ins count for nothing. Rosters stay the same size: in a 2-for-1 the team getting two players drops its weakest player, and the team getting one picks up the best free agent for the open spot. Both count toward the verdict, lineup changes and ranks.
